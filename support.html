Common questions

How do I unlock all the topics?

Tap any topic with a lock, then Show a grown-up. Or open Settings (the gear at the top right) and choose For grown-ups. Answer the grown-up question, then tap Buy. It is a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.

I already bought it. How do I get it back?

Open For grown-ups and tap Restore purchase. Make sure the iPad is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. This also works after deleting and reinstalling the app, or on a new iPad.

Can other people in my family use it?

If you use Family Sharing, family members can unlock it on their own devices with Restore purchase, without buying again.

My child pressed Buy and nothing happened.

If Ask to Buy is on, the request goes to the parent's device for approval. Once it is approved, the topics unlock by themselves.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app and works offline. Only buying or restoring the purchase needs a connection.

The voice is off, or the sound is too loud.

Open Settings (the gear) to turn sound on or off. For the youngest age level, the read-aloud switch is at the top right of each topic.

How do I change the language or the age level?

Open Settings (the gear) on the home screen. The three age levels (5–7, 8–10, 11–13) are only a guide; any child can use any level.

帮助

请写信到 stubbornleaf@gmail.com，告诉我们 iPad 型号、系统版本和遇到的问题。

怎么解锁全部主题？点任意一个带锁的主题，再点“给大人看”；或者打开右上角的设置，选“家长区”。回答家长验证的问题后点“购买”。一次购买，没有订阅。

已经买过，怎么找回？打开“家长区”，点“恢复购买”。请确认 iPad 登录的是购买时用的 Apple 账户。删除重装或换新 iPad 也一样。

家人能一起用吗？开启了家人共享的话，家人在自己的设备上点“恢复购买”就能解锁，不用再买。

孩子点了购买没反应？如果开了“购买前询问”，请求会发到家长的设备上，批准后会自动解锁。

需要联网吗？不需要。只有购买和恢复购买需要联网。

退款：购买由 Apple 处理，请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。
