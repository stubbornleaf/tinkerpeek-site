TinkerPeek is an iPad app for children aged about 5 to 8. Open up a rocket, an eye, a volcano or a washing machine in 3D, watch how it works step by step, then try it yourself: tune it, drag it, fix it.

50 topics across machines, home, digital things, the body, the Earth and space. 8 are free to try, and one purchase unlocks all 50.

No ads. No subscription. No accounts. No data collected. Everything works offline.

English and Chinese, and every card can be read aloud.

Questions? See Support or read our Privacy Policy.
