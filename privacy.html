Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

In short: TinkerPeek does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

What the app collects

Nothing. TinkerPeek does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, ages, birthdays, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage statistics. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

What stays on your iPad

To work properly, the app remembers a few settings on the device itself: the language, the age level, the home page view, sound and read-aloud on or off, and which parts, goals and stars a child has already found. This information never leaves the iPad, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made on this device, so it can unlock the topics. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check inside the app.

Internet

All topics, pictures and recordings are inside the app. The app does not load anything from the internet while a child uses it. The only time it talks to a server is when a grown-up buys or restores the purchase, and that conversation is between the device and Apple.

Children's privacy

TinkerPeek is made for children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13, and there is nothing for parents to review or delete. This is our approach under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the App Store's rules for the Kids category.

Links

The only link out of the app, to this policy, is behind the grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. We will not start collecting personal information without changing this policy first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: stubbornleaf@gmail.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

简单说：TinkerPeek 不收集、不保存、不分享任何个人信息。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计和追踪，断网也能用。

收集什么：什么都不收集。不要姓名、邮箱、年龄、生日、照片、录音、位置、通讯录、设备标识或使用统计，也没有任何第三方统计、广告或追踪工具。

留在 iPad 上的：为了正常使用，应用只在本机记住几项设置：语言、年龄段、首页样式、声音和朗读开关，以及孩子已经发现的零件、完成的目标和星星。这些不会离开 iPad，不会发给我们或任何人，删除应用时一起删除。

购买：唯一的内购完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们收不到你的姓名、Apple 账户或支付信息。应用只向 Apple 确认这台设备是否已经购买，用来解锁主题。购买放在应用里的“家长验证”之后。

联网：所有主题、图片和录音都在应用里，孩子使用时不从网上加载任何东西。只有大人购买或恢复购买时，设备会和 Apple 通信。

儿童隐私：应用是给孩子用的。我们不收集任何人的个人信息，所以也不收集 13 岁以下儿童的信息，没有需要家长查看或删除的数据。

变更：如有变更，我们会更新本页和上面的日期；在改变本政策之前，绝不会开始收集个人信息。
